Price Recommender - Interactive Demo

Companion demo for GrayRobert/Automated-Machine-Learning-Price-Recommender (BSc thesis, 2019). Train regression models in your browser on the bundled synthetic sample, or upload your own CSV and build a predictor from it, using scikit-learn compiled to WebAssembly (Pyodide). Everything runs locally on your device: files you choose are read by your browser only and are never uploaded or sent anywhere.

Demo data only. The 3,000 sample rows are synthetic, generated from a fixed formula plus noise (seed fixed for reproducibility). They do not represent real packages, customers, or prices.

1. Choose your data

Try it instantly on the sample, or upload a CSV where each row is one record (e.g. one past sale) and each column a field.

Limits: CSV up to 10 MB and 25,000 rows, up to 50 columns. Larger files: take a random sample of your rows first. This tool predicts a number (regression); it does not classify categories or forecast time series.

2. Choose what to predict

Pick the numeric column to predict (the target) and the columns to predict it from. Text columns with many distinct values (IDs, dates, free text) are unticked automatically because they rarely generalise; you can tick them back on.

Trains Linear Regression, Decision Tree and Random Forest on an 80/20 split; metrics are computed on the held-out test set.
