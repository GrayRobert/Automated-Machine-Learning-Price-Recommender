Price Recommender - Interactive Demo

Companion demo for GrayRobert/Automated-Machine-Learning-Price-Recommender (BSc thesis, 2019). Three regression models train in your browser on synthetic holiday-package data using scikit-learn compiled to WebAssembly (Pyodide). Training and prediction run locally on the bundled synthetic sample data; no user data is uploaded or collected.

Demo data only. The 3,000 training rows are synthetic, generated from a fixed formula plus noise (seed fixed for reproducibility). They do not represent real packages, customers, or prices, and results here demonstrate the training workflow, not production accuracy.

1. Train the models

Linear Regression, Decision Tree and Random Forest are trained on an 80/20 split. Metrics are computed on the held-out test set after training.
